Programme Overview

The Higher National Diploma (HND) in Computing is equivalent to the second year of a UK bachelor's degree. It combines strong theory with practical, industry-focused projects.

Students learn in modern labs with experienced tutors, and can progress to a one-year top-up for a full UK honours degree.

Software Development

Programming, OOP and application development.

Networking

Design and manage secure networks.

Cyber Security

Risk, threats and security management.

Databases

Database design, SQL and data analysis.

Complete a one-year top-up locally or at a UK partner campus to earn a full honours degree.

Entry Requirements

  • HSC / A' Level with passes in at least 2 subjects, or a National Diploma
  • IELTS 5.5 or the Meridian internal English test
  • Mature applicants with relevant experience are welcome

Career Opportunities

Graduates work as software developers, network administrators, web developers, IT support specialists and security analysts, or continue to an honours degree.
