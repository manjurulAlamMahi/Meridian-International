Who we are

Welcome to Meridian International

Meridian International Study Centre believes in making world-class education accessible to every motivated learner. Our philosophy is built on curiosity, independent thinking and practical skills that prepare students for a successful global career.

Established in January 2010, Meridian acts as a learning facilitator for UK universities and awarding bodies. Students study the same curriculum and receive the same internationally recognised qualifications as in the UK, at a fraction of the cost of studying abroad.

“Our goal is simple: give every student the confidence and qualifications to compete anywhere in the world.”
Chairman, Meridian International (demo)
Our Vision

To be the most trusted gateway to UK higher education in Bangladesh.

Our Mission

To deliver affordable, high-quality UK programmes that create employable graduates.

Our Values

Integrity, excellence, inclusivity and genuine care for every learner.
